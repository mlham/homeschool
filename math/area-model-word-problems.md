# Area Model Adventures: Word Problems

## Ready to Explore?
Grab your pencil and imagination! These word problems use the break apart rule to solve multiplication stories about robots, rockets, and sports. Use area models to keep track of every part of the story and crack the answers like a math adventurer.

## How to Use Area Models
1. Break apart the numbers into tens and ones.
2. Draw a rectangle and label the parts.
3. Find each smaller area.
4. Add the smaller answers to solve the story.

## Guided Word Problems (With Answers)
Try to solve each problem before reading the answer.

### 1. Racing Robots
KJ builds 24 mini racing robots. Each robot uses 3 glowing gears. How many glowing gears does KJ need?

Area model setup:
```text
+--------+----+
|  20    |  4 |
+--------+----+
```

Smaller areas:
- 20 × 3 = 60 gears
- 4 × 3 = 12 gears

Add them: 60 + 12 = **72 gears**

### 2. Rocket Sticker Packs
Jordan makes sticker packs with rocket ships. Each pack has 18 stickers. His club needs 14 packs for a space show. How many stickers will Jordan bring?

Area model setup:
```text
         10        4
      +--------+-------+
 10   | 10×10 | 10×4  |
      +--------+-------+
  8   | 8×10  | 8×4   |
      +--------+-------+
```

Smaller areas:
- 10 × 10 = 100 stickers
- 10 × 4 = 40 stickers
- 8 × 10 = 80 stickers
- 8 × 4 = 32 stickers

Add them: 100 + 40 + 80 + 32 = **252 stickers**

### 3. Slam Dunk Practice
Chris practices basketball. He shoots 23 balls each day for 16 days. How many shots does Chris take?

Area model setup:
```text
         10        6
      +--------+-------+
 20   | 20×10 | 20×6  |
      +--------+-------+
  3   | 3×10  | 3×6   |
      +--------+-------+
```

Smaller areas:
- 20 × 10 = 200 shots
- 20 × 6 = 120 shots
- 3 × 10 = 30 shots
- 3 × 6 = 18 shots

Add them: 200 + 120 + 30 + 18 = **368 shots**

## Practice Word Problems (You Try!)
Solve each story with an area model. Show every smaller area before adding.

1. Tyler builds 17 toy rockets. Each rocket has 5 fuel cells. How many fuel cells does he use?
2. Max stacks 26 comic books on each shelf. He fills 7 shelves. How many comic books are on the shelves?
3. A soccer team sells 32 bags of popcorn each game. They play 11 games. How many bags do they sell?
4. Leo paints 28 skateboard decks. Each deck uses 13 lightning stickers. How many stickers does he need?
5. A game store orders 36 boxes of trading cards. Each box holds 14 packs. How many packs arrive?
