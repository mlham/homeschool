# Multi-Digit Multiplication with Area Models

## Warm Welcome

Today we will learn how to use area models to multiply bigger numbers. Area models help us see how a big rectangle can be broken into smaller rectangles that are easier to solve. Get your pencil ready, and let's have some fun!

## What Is the Break Apart Rule?

When we use an area model, we use the break apart rule. This means we split one number into tens and ones, draw a rectangle, and label each part. Then we find the smaller answers and add them together for the final answer.

## Step-by-Step: One-Digit × Two-Digit (23 × 4)

1. Break apart 23 into 20 and 3.
2. Draw a wide rectangle and split it into two parts:

   ```text
   +---------+----+
   |  20     |  3 |
   +---------+----+
   ```

3. Write 4 along the side to show 4 rows.
4. Find the smaller areas:
   - Left rectangle: 20 × 4 = 80
   - Right rectangle: 3 × 4 = 12
5. Add the smaller answers: 80 + 12 = 92
6. So, 23 × 4 = **92**

## Step-by-Step: Two-Digit × Two-Digit (23 × 15)

1. Break apart 23 into 20 and 3.
2. Break apart 15 into 10 and 5.
3. Draw a rectangle split into four parts:

   ```text
           10        5
        +--------+-------+
   20   | 20×10 | 20×5  |
        +--------+-------+
    3   | 3×10  | 3×5   |
        +--------+-------+
   ```

4. Find each smaller area (partial product):
   - Top left: 20 × 10 = 200
   - Top right: 20 × 5 = 100
   - Bottom left: 3 × 10 = 30
   - Bottom right: 3 × 5 = 15
5. Add the smaller answers: 200 + 100 + 30 + 15 = 345
6. So, 23 × 15 = **345**

## Guided Practice (With Answers)

Let's solve these together. Try first, then check.

1. **16 × 3**  
   - Break apart 16 into 10 and 6.  
   - 10 × 3 = 30  
   - 6 × 3 = 18  
   - Final answer: **48**

2. **24 × 12**  
   - Break apart 24 into 20 and 4. Break apart 12 into 10 and 2.  
   - 20 × 10 = 200  
   - 20 × 2 = 40  
   - 4 × 10 = 40  
   - 4 × 2 = 8  
   - Final answer: **288**

3. **31 × 14**  
   - Break apart 31 into 30 and 1. Break apart 14 into 10 and 4.  
   - 30 × 10 = 300  
   - 30 × 4 = 120  
   - 1 × 10 = 10  
   - 1 × 4 = 4  
   - Final answer: **434**
