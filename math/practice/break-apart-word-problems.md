# Break Apart Word Problems

Read each story, break apart the bigger number, and solve by adding the smaller answers. Leave the final answer blank so you can fill it in.

1. **Skatepark Snacks**  
   There are 3 snack stands at the skatepark. Each stand sells 14 smoothies on Saturday. How many smoothies are sold in all?  
   Show your split: 3 × 14 = (3 × 10) + (3 × 4) = ____

2. **Comic Book Club**  
   A club has 5 tables. Each table holds 18 comic books. How many comic books are on all the tables?  
   Show your split: 5 × 18 = (5 × 10) + (5 × 8) = ____

3. **Robot Race Practice**  
   4 friends each practice 16 laps with their racing robots. How many laps do the robots run altogether?  
   Show your split: 4 × 16 = (4 × 10) + (4 × 6) = ____

4. **Soccer Goal Challenge**  
   The soccer team plays 2 games this week. They score 17 goals in each game. How many goals do they score altogether?  
   Show your split: 2 × 17 = (2 × 10) + (2 × 7) = ____

5. **Treehouse Supplies**  
   Mateo fills 6 boxes with 12 wooden planks each for the treehouse. How many planks does he pack?  
   Show your split: 6 × 12 = (6 × 10) + (6 × 2) = ____

6. **Dino Dig Tickets**  
   The museum sells 7 sets of tickets for the Dino Dig event. Each set has 13 tickets. How many tickets are sold?  
   Show your split: 7 × 13 = (7 × 10) + (7 × 3) = ____

7. **Arcade Tokens**  
   Jay buys 8 rolls of arcade tokens. Each roll has 15 tokens. How many tokens does Jay have?

8. **Science Kit Orders**  
   Two classrooms order 19 science kits each. How many science kits are ordered?

9. **Skateboard Stickers**  
   A shop gives 4 sticker sheets with 17 stickers on each sheet to every customer. How many stickers does each customer get?  
   Show your split: 4 × 17 = (4 × 10) + (4 × 7) = ____

10. **Space Camp Backpacks**
